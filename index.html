About

A technically expert, learner-focused Lecturer in Computing, Engineering and AI, with proven UK Further Education and Higher Education teaching experience across Networking, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Robotics, Electronics, and Artificial Intelligence.

Holds an MSc in Artificial Intelligence with Robotics (London Metropolitan University, 2025) and a BEng in Instrumentation & Control Engineering, combining academic depth with hands-on industry practice in embedded systems, machine learning model development, PCB design, and network security.

Consistently designs and delivers Pearson BTEC HND Level 4/5 curricula, integrates industry-standard ILT tools (Cisco Packet Tracer, Wireshark, TensorFlow, VMware), and embeds inclusive, learner-centred pedagogy through Universal Design for Learning and SEN/autism-adapted instruction.

A research-active practitioner who brings live engineering and AI projects directly into the classroom — with a strong record of meeting retention, achievement and success targets while supporting non-traditional and widening-participation learners.
